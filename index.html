About Us

Sri Krishna Group

Jangaon, Telangana, India.

SK Groups is a professionally managed academic and scholarly publishing imprint operating under Sri Krishna Groups,a registered partnership firm based in Jangaon,Telangana, India. We are dedicated to the publication and dissemination of high-quality academic,research, and professional knowledge. Our objective is to support authors, researchers, academicians,institutions,and professional bodies by providing a transparent,ethical, and efficient publishing platform that adheres to national and international academic standards.

Scope of Publishing

SK Groups specializes in publishing scholarly and general works in the following domains: Agriculture and Allied Sciences, Biotechnology and Life Sciences, Natural Resource Management, Climate Change and Sustainability, Environmental Sciences, Basic and Applied Sciences, Social Sciences, Education, Humanities, Novels and Fiction, Poetry and Literary Works (English & Telugu).

ABOUT US

Sri Krishna Groups is a registered partnership firm established with the vision of contributing to education, research, and scholarly communication. The publishing activities of the firm are carried out through its academic imprint, SK Groups.

SK Groups was founded to meet the increasing demand for credible, ethical, and academically sound publishing avenues in India. We aim to bridge the gap between knowledge creators and knowledge seekers by making scholarly work accessible, reliable, and professionally published.

Vision

To become a trusted and respected academic publishing platform that promotes research excellence and knowledge dissemination.

Mission

  • To publish original and high-quality academic and research content
  • To encourage interdisciplinary and applied research
  • To maintain high ethical and editorial standards
  • To support early-career researchers and academicians

Core Values

  • Academic integrity
  • Transparency and fairness
  • Professional excellence
  • Ethical responsibility

PUBLICATIONS

SK Groups publishes a wide range of scholarly, academic, and general-interest materials, including but not limited to:

  • Academic textbooks for undergraduate and postgraduate courses
  • Reference books and handbooks
  • Edited volumes and contributed books
  • Books arising out of conferences and seminars
  • Novels, short story collections, and general books
  • Poetry and literary works (English & Telugu)
  • Institutional, departmental, and commemorative publications

Each publication undergoes editorial review, formatting, and quality checks to ensure academic credibility and compliance with publishing norms.

CALL FOR BOOK CHAPTERS (EDITED BOOKS)

SK Groups regularly invites original and unpublished book chapters from academicians, researchers, scientists, professionals, and subject experts for inclusion in edited books on contemporary and emerging themes.

About Edited Books

Edited books published by SK Groups are thematic volumes curated by expert editors. Each volume brings together high-quality chapters addressing specific research areas, practical applications, and emerging trends.

Chapter Submission Guidelines

  • Chapters must be original, unpublished, and not under consideration elsewhere
  • Plagiarism must be within acceptable academic limits
  • Chapter length: as specified in the individual call
  • Format: MS Word (DOC/DOCX)
  • References must follow a standard academic citation style

Submission Email: submissions@skgroups.in

AUTHOR CORNER

SK Groups welcomes manuscript submissions from academicians, researchers, scientists, and professionals from India and abroad.

Author Guidelines

  • Manuscripts must be original, unpublished, and not under consideration elsewhere
  • All submissions must be plagiarism-free
  • Manuscripts should follow standard academic structure
  • Preferred format: DOC or DOCX

Submission

Email: skgroups230126@gmail.com

Required: Author name(s), affiliation(s), contact details, manuscript title, manuscript file (DOC/DOCX), declaration of originality.

CONFERENCES & CONFERENCE-BASED BOOKS

SK Groups collaborates with universities, colleges, research institutions, and professional bodies to publish edited books developed through academic conferences, seminars, and workshops. Conference outputs are published only as ISBN-assigned edited books. SK Groups does not publish stand-alone abstract books or souvenirs.

Publication Ethics

SK Groups follows strict ethical publishing standards. Plagiarism, data fabrication, falsification, duplicate submission, or copyright violation is not permitted.
